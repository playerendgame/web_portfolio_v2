CNI CRM

Pipeline, targets and the engineering handoff for commercial and industrial solar projects.

Period
2024 to now
My role
Sole developer, with one coworker on later modules
Built with
Laravel, Vue.js, MySQL, Chart.js, SortableJS, Excel export

The problem

Commercial solar deals take months and pass through site visits, technical assessment, quotation, engineering and installation. Sales tracked them in spreadsheets, so management could not see the pipeline or measure it against monthly targets.

What I built

A pipeline people actually update

Deals move between configurable stages by drag and drop. Each card shows the amount, account manager and sales engineer, and turns into an idle warning after 7 and 15 days without a call, note or site visit. Moving a card does not count as activity, so the warning cannot be cleared by dragging.

Targets and a dashboard management reads

Monthly targets, achievement rate, year-to-date conversion and active pipeline, switchable between pesos and kWp, filtered by engineer or business unit, over any period from 1 to 12 months.

Handoff to engineering and procurement

Deals in the bill-of-quantities stage go to engineering, who attach their references and pass the deal to procurement. Sales engineers are assigned round robin, and accounts are created from Track when someone is granted the CRM.

The source code belongs to my employer and is private. I'm happy to walk through the architecture and code on a call.
