GoPrime Track

One portal for HR attendance, IT, fleet, training and marketing reports, and the single sign-in for the other company apps.

Period
2025 to now
My role
Sole developer: data model, backend, frontend, office services and deployment
Built with
Laravel, Vue.js, MySQL, Python, Docker, OAuth 2.0 with PKCE, SNMPv3, Meta Graph API

The problem

Each department ran on its own spreadsheet, paper form or vendor tool, and every new app meant another set of accounts to manage. The first ERP covered warehouse, accounting and helpdesk requests. The company needed one place for everything else, and one sign-in for all of it, so the ERP modules moved into Track.

What I built

One sign-in for every app

Track is the identity provider for the other company apps. They send people to Track to sign in through an OAuth authorization code flow with PKCE. Granting someone an app in Track creates their account there automatically, and revoking it deactivates the account without deleting their records. Each push is signed with HMAC so nothing else can call it.

Attendance from fingerprint scanners

Six scanners across three sites feed HR. The cloud server cannot reach the office network, so a small Python service in Docker runs on an office PC, reads every scanner every few seconds and pushes new taps to Track over HTTPS. It catches up after outages, restarts the vendor bridge when it stalls, and lets IT reboot a scanner from Track. HR exports attendance in the 29-column sheet they already used, and the calculation matches their own file on about 99.7% of rows.

Network monitoring for the firewalls

A second collector reads the FortiGate firewall through its REST API and the Sophos firewall through SNMPv3 every minute: CPU, memory, VPN tunnels, both internet lines and Wi-Fi access points. Track keeps per-minute readings for 7 days and hourly rollups for 400 days, opens an incident when a line or tunnel stays down, and generates the IT team’s incident report in their spreadsheet layout. The gauges and charts are SVG components I wrote, with no chart library.

Marketing reports from Meta

Scheduled jobs fetch paid ads, organic posts, followers and competitor numbers for every brand from Meta’s Graph API, and the reports compare any two periods. When Meta refuses a large request, the job retries in smaller date ranges. Access tokens stay on the server.

The everyday modules

Access control by module and action, projects and tasks, leave and meetings with conflict checks, fleet with live GPS tracking, IT assets with loans and tag status, IT ticketing with a public submit page, a property registry, and a training platform with quizzes, essay review, video progress that cannot be skipped, and certificates.

The source code belongs to my employer and is private. I'm happy to walk through the architecture and code on a call.
