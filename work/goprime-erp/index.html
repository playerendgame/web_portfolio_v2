The problem

Material requests, payment requests and IT problems went around on paper and in chat, with no record of who approved what. Each company brand needed its own data on the same system.

What I built

Requests with an approval trail

Warehouse material requests (MPRF) and accounting payment requests (RFP) moved to tracked digital approvals, with each brand’s data kept separate on shared infrastructure.

Helpdesk and project tracking

An IT ticketing desk with priority and support tiers, and a project tracker with board, calendar, Gantt and workload views. Both later became modules of Track.

The source code belongs to my employer and is private. I'm happy to walk through the architecture and code on a call.
